2025

Postcards - Pro-Bono

Rome, Italy

Exploring the collective fear of being forgotten, this group exhibtion in Parco Degli Acquedotti featured an interactive installation of postcards hung between trees. As visitors took the cards, new ones were added, creating a performance centered on the exchange of visibility, connection and memory.

2026

Gjord för att älska ("Made to love")

Uppsala, Sweden

Drawing inspiration from a formative period spent in Rome, Mirlind presents his first solo exhibition that explores the profound tension and harmony between classical aesthetics and homoeroticism. The series investigates how queer identity and desire can claim space in environments where they have traditionally been rendered invisible.
